X.509 + ZK Proofs

Sign Anonymize Verify

1. Sign

Standard C2PA signature (ES256, P-384 CA chain) with a time stamp, as on a real device

2. Anonymize

A third party replaces the signature with a Groth16 proof (gnark), without the signer's key; optionally hide the issuer and the time stamp

3. Verify

Verify the proof, the claim, and the hard binding against the trust lists
